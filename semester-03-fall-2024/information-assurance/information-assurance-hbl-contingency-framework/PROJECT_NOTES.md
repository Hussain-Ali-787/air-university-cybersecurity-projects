# Project Notes

## Project Name

Information Assurance Semester Project: A Robust Contingency Framework for Habib Bank Limited (HBL)

## Purpose

The purpose of this project is to design a practical contingency framework for a banking organization. The project demonstrates how compliance, risk management, business continuity, incident response, and disaster recovery can be combined into one structured information assurance plan.

## Why HBL Was Selected

HBL was selected because it is a major banking-sector organization with critical customer-facing and internal services. Banking services require high availability, secure processing, regulatory compliance, and effective recovery planning.

## Main Topics Covered

- Compliance framework analysis
- Risk assessment
- Compliance monitoring and auditing
- Employee training and awareness
- Contingency planning
- Incident Response Plan
- Business Continuity Plan
- Disaster Recovery Plan

## Standards and Frameworks Referenced

- State Bank of Pakistan guidelines
- ISO/IEC 27001
- PCI DSS
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework

## Key Risks Considered

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cybersecurity breach | High | Medium | High |
| Hardware or data center failure | High | Medium | High |
| Natural disaster | High | Low | Medium |
| Network downtime | Medium | High | High |

## Critical Services Considered

| Service | Reason for Criticality |
|---|---|
| Core banking systems | Required for banking operations and transaction processing |
| Online banking | Required for customer access and digital services |
| Payment processing | Required for financial transactions |
| Customer service helplines | Required for customer support during disruptions |

## Important Design Decisions

The framework uses a layered structure:

1. Prevention and monitoring through compliance and risk management
2. Response through the Incident Response Plan
3. Continuity through the Business Continuity Plan
4. Recovery through the Disaster Recovery Plan
5. Improvement through training, auditing, and testing

## Limitations

This project is based on academic research and publicly understandable banking-sector requirements. It does not use confidential or internal HBL documents. Some assumptions were made for educational purposes, especially around RTO, RPO, backup strategies, and audit findings.
