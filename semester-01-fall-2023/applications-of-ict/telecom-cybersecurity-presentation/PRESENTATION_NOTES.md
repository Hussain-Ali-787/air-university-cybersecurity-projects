## Main Idea

Telecommunication networks are critical infrastructure. They support communication, business, internet connectivity, emergency services, and digital life. Because telecom companies manage sensitive customer data and large-scale infrastructure, cybersecurity is essential.

## Key Takeaways

- Telecom networks must be protected as critical infrastructure.
- Customer data protection is a major responsibility of telecom companies.
- Cybersecurity supports trust, service availability, and national security.
- Telecom operators must combine technology, policy, awareness, and incident response.
