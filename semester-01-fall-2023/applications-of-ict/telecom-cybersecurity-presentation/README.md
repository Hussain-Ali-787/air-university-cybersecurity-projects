## Key Concepts

### Telecommunication

Telecommunication refers to the transmission of voice, data, and video over long distances using electronic means. It includes communication through phones, mobile networks, the internet, instant messaging, video conferencing, and social media.

### Why Cybersecurity Matters in Telecom

Telecom networks are critical because they support:

- National communication infrastructure
- Business communication
- Emergency communication
- Internet access
- Customer identity and billing data
- Mobile and digital services

A successful attack on telecom infrastructure can disrupt communication services, expose customer data, damage trust, and create national security risks.

---

## Common Cyberattacks in Telecom

| Attack Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Data Breach | Unauthorized exposure or theft of customer information |
| DDoS Attack | Overwhelming telecom services with traffic |
| Phishing | Deceptive messages used to steal credentials |
| Man-in-the-Middle | Interception of communication between users |
| Malware | Malicious software used to steal data or disrupt systems |
| SIM Card Cloning | Copying SIM identity to misuse telecom services |
| Insider Threat | Misuse of access by employees or trusted users |

---

## Impact of Cyberattacks

Cyberattacks against telecom systems can cause:

- Service outages
- Loss of connectivity
- Emergency communication disruption
- Customer data exposure
- Regulatory penalties
- Financial losses
- Reputation damage
- Increased recovery and security costs
