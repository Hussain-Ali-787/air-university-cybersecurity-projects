## Overview

This folder contains coursework completed for **Applications of Information and Communication Technologies** during Semester 1 of the BS Cyber Security program at Air University Islamabad.

The course introduced foundational ICT concepts, digital communication, web-based tools, presentation development, and practical use of online platforms. The included projects demonstrate both theoretical understanding and hands-on application through a cybersecurity-focused presentation and a website development task.
